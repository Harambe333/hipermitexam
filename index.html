Before you take the real exam

  • The Hawaiʻi knowledge test is 30 questions; you must score 80% (24 correct) to pass.
  • You must be at least 15½ years old to apply for an instruction permit.
  • Bring proof of identity, legal presence, social security number, and two proofs of Hawaiʻi residence.
  • If you're under 18, you'll need a parent or guardian's consent and you must hold the permit 180 days before applying for a provisional license.
